# Klaus Behnam Shad

Anthropologist building reproducible, non-black-box research software for qualitative data and AI governance analysis.

## Research Orientation

My work develops meta-analytical frameworks that translate sociological and anthropological logics into structured, transparent computational environments.

I focus on:

- Relational modeling of social differentiation and justice
- Narrative and positional analysis
- Socio-cybernetic perspectives on governance and AI systems
- Rule-based analytical infrastructures for qualitative research
- Reproducibility and epistemic traceability in interpretative methodologies

## Conceptual Approach

Rather than automating interpretation, I design analytical architectures that preserve theoretical clarity, methodological transparency, and structural accountability.

My broader aim is to operationalize sociological reasoning without reducing it to black-box computation.
